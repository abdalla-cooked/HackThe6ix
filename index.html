Haunted Files

Demons are possessing your files. Save them with your hands:

  1. A possessed file shakes and glows green, with its monster sitting on top.
  2. Pinch it (thumb and index together), drag it and let go over the cauldron. The demon is banished.
  3. Clear the level, then hold a thumbs up for one second to start the next one.

Mouse: left drag = pinch, hold P = open palm, hold T = thumbs up, hold V = pause. Spell signs: F fist, D point, P palm, H horns. M mutes.

Best with sound on, in a dark room. Scare level: Nightmare
