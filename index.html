Haunted Files

Demons are possessing your files. Save them with your hands:

  1. A possessed file shakes and glows green, with its monster sitting on top.
  2. Pinch it (thumb and index together) and drop it into the cauldron.
  3. Point one finger and draw the monster's spell shape in the air.

Mouse: left drag = pinch, right drag or hold D = draw, hold P = open palm, hold T = thumb up.
